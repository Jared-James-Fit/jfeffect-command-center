The JF Effect app

Everything your training needs, in one app.

Programs

Your program, on the days you train.

Follow one of my 15 programs or build your own. The workouts land on your training days, and when your week changes, you move them.

Logging

Log a set in seconds.

Reps and RPE come filled in from the program. Type the weight and it's logged, with last time on the same card.

Progress

See what's working.

Estimated maxes, PRs, volume and consistency, worked out from every set you log.

Nutrition

Eat for your goal.

Get your calories and macros, build a meal plan from foods you like, and log what you eat.

Your own program

Build one, or bring the one you have.

Either way it's tracked like my programs: every set, PR and week.

Build your own

Pick how many weeks and days, add exercises from the library, and set the sets, reps, RPE and load. Change one week or every week at once.

Save it, start it, and it lands on your calendar like any of mine.

Upload one you already have

A spreadsheet, a Word doc, a PDF or photos of it. It comes in with its weeks, days and numbers, matched to the exercise library, and you check every line before it's saved.

Most files come in within seconds. PDFs and photos take a little longer. What you upload stays private to you.

  • Bar speed on any set, if you train with a velocity tracker
  • Pause or switch programs and pick up where you left off

Online coaching with Jared McIntyre

I'll run your training.

I write your program and nutrition around your goal, your schedule and your equipment. Every week I read your check-in, go through what you logged, and decide what changes. Your job is to train and report back.

Your program
Written for your goal, your schedule and your equipment, and changed as you progress.
Weekly check-ins
You check in from the app. I go through it with your numbers and tell you what's changing and why.
Nutrition
Calories, macros and a meal plan built around the food you eat, updated as your bodyweight moves.
Technique and questions
Film a set and I see it with its weight, reps and RPE, plus the bar path and speed the app reads from the video. Ask me anything in our chat. I usually reply within a day.
Meet prep
If you compete, we plan your season, your peak and your weight class together.
