## A. Independent Coach Platform Agreement

> **1. Who this is between.** This agreement is between you (the coach, and
> the business you operate, if any) and Jared James Fit / JF Effect ("JF
> Effect", "we"). It applies when you create a coaching organization in JF
> Effect to run your own coaching business. It's in addition to the JF Effect
> Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
>
> **2. What JF Effect provides.** Software to run your coaching business:
> client management, program and nutrition builders, check-ins, messaging,
> scheduling, the AI assistant (Cleo) and, if you connect it, payment
> processing through Stripe. We provide the software only. We don't coach
> your clients, supervise you, review your programs or advice, or guarantee
> any result.
>
> **3. Your business is yours.** You run an independent business. You are not
> JF Effect's employee, contractor, agent or partner, and you won't present
> yourself as one. You decide what services you offer, to whom, how and at
> what price, and you are responsible for:
> (a) the coaching, programs, nutrition guidance and other services you give
> your clients, and the results of following them;
> (b) holding the qualifications, certifications, registrations and
> insurance your services and your province require, and only using a title
> you're entitled to use (see 7);
> (c) your agreements with your clients, including your own terms, waivers,
> refund and cancellation policies;
> (d) your pricing, invoicing, taxes (including GST/HST registration and
> remittance) and bookkeeping;
> (e) complying with the laws that apply to your business, including consumer
> protection, privacy and anti-spam laws.
>
> **4. Your clients.** Your clients use JF Effect under the Terms of Service
> and Privacy Policy. Their training, nutrition and health records belong to
> them. Your organization can see and change what they share with you while
> they're your client. When the coaching relationship ends, your access to
> their records ends; their records stay theirs. We won't give your clients'
> information to another coach or to JF Effect's own coaching business, and
> we won't contact your clients to sell them JF Effect coaching. Your
> clients may still use JF Effect's free features and community, and may
> choose to work with another coach.
>
> **5. Your data and our role.** For your clients' personal information you
> collect through JF Effect, you decide why it's collected and how it's used,
> and JF Effect processes it on your behalf as your service provider under the
> Data Processing Addendum (Schedule C). You are responsible for having a
> lawful basis and the consents you need, for your own privacy notice to your
> clients, and for answering their requests about information you hold
> outside JF Effect.
>
> **6. Payments.** If you connect a Stripe account, payments from your
> clients go to your Stripe account, under Stripe's own agreement with you.
> You are the merchant: refunds, disputes, chargebacks, failed payments and
> taxes on your sales are yours. JF Effect never holds your clients' payments
> and never sees your full card or bank details. [If a platform fee applies:
> JF Effect charges a platform fee of [x]% per payment / $[x] per month,
> shown before you connect and on every payout statement. No other fee is
> taken from your clients' payments.]
>
> **7. Professional limits.** You won't diagnose or treat medical conditions,
> prescribe medication or give advice that requires a licence you don't hold.
> You'll only use a protected professional title (for example "dietitian",
> and in some provinces "nutritionist", "physiotherapist" or "kinesiologist")
> if you're registered to use it where you and your client are. You'll refer a
> client to a qualified health professional when their situation calls for it
> (for example an injury, a medical condition, pregnancy, an eating disorder,
> or medication that affects training or nutrition).
>
> **8. Cleo and other AI features.** Cleo and other AI features help you draft
> programs, nutrition plans, messages and summaries. Their output is a draft
> for you to check. You are responsible for anything you send to a client or
> publish, whether you wrote it or Cleo drafted it. Cleo only acts with your
> permissions and asks you to confirm before it changes a client's plan,
> sends a message, or does anything with money or access. Cleo isn't a
> doctor, dietitian or lawyer and doesn't replace one.
>
> **9. Acceptable use.** The Terms of Service's acceptable-use rules apply to
> you and your organization. In addition, you won't: upload content you don't
> have the rights to (see D); use JF Effect to send messages your clients
> didn't agree to receive; try to access another organization's data; or use
> JF Effect's programs, templates or content outside JF Effect except as the
> Terms allow.
>
> **10. Content you create.** You own the programs, templates, recipes and
> other content you create. You give JF Effect a licence to store, process and
> show it only as needed to run the service for you and the people you share
> it with. We don't use it to train AI models or in our marketing without
> your separate written permission. JF Effect's own programs, templates and
> content stay ours; you may use them with your clients inside JF Effect.
>
> **11. Fees for JF Effect.** [Subscription price, billing interval, taxes,
> what happens on non-payment, and how we give notice of a price change.]
>
> **12. Suspension and ending.** You can close your organization at any time.
> We may suspend or close it on notice for a serious or repeated breach,
> unpaid fees, or where the law requires, and immediately where your use puts
> clients, other users or the platform at risk. When it closes: your
> clients keep their accounts and records; you can export your organization's
> records for [30] days; after that we delete or de-identify what we hold for
> your organization, except records we must keep by law (for example
> financial and tax records) or to resolve a dispute.
>
> **13. Liability.** To the extent permitted by law: JF Effect isn't
> responsible for your services to your clients or for the results of
> following programs, advice or AI drafts you provide; and JF Effect's total
> liability to you under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid JF
> Effect in the 12 months before the claim. Nothing in this agreement limits
> liability that can't be limited by law (for example for fraud, or for
> death or personal injury caused by negligence where the law doesn't allow
> it to be limited), or any right a consumer has that can't be waived.
>
> **14. Indemnity.** To the extent permitted by law, you'll cover JF Effect's
> reasonable costs (including legal fees) of a claim by one of your clients
> or a third party that arises from your services, your content, your breach
> of this agreement, or your breach of law, except to the extent the claim
> was caused by JF Effect.
>
> **15. Changes.** We'll give you at least [30] days' notice of a material
> change to this agreement in the app and by email. If you don't agree, you
> can close your organization before it takes effect.
>
> **16. Law.** This agreement is governed by the laws of Manitoba and the
> federal laws of Canada that apply there.

## B. JF Effect Coach Agreement

For a coach the owner invites into the JF Effect coaching organization.
Read together with any employment or contractor agreement; if they conflict,
that agreement wins on pay and employment terms, and this one on platform
access and client data.

> **1. Access.** JF Effect gives you a coach login to JF Effect's coaching
> organization. What you can see and do is set by the owner (your role, the
> clients assigned to you, and any extra permissions) and can change at any
> time. Access ends immediately when the owner revokes it or your engagement
> ends.
>
> **2. Clients and their records belong to JF Effect's clients.** The clients
> you coach are JF Effect's coaching clients. Their records, and the
> programs and plans written for them in JF Effect, stay in JF Effect when you
> leave. You won't copy, export or take client records, contact details or
> JF Effect's programs and templates outside JF Effect, during or after your
> engagement, except as the owner permits in writing. [Non-solicitation:
> confirm scope, duration and enforceability with counsel; Manitoba courts
> read these narrowly.]
>
> **3. Confidentiality.** JF Effect's business information you see (clients'
> personal and health information, prices, business systems, AI instructions,
> templates not yet published) is confidential. You use it only to coach JF
> Effect's clients and keep it confidential during and after your
> engagement.
>
> **4. Privacy.** You handle clients' personal information under JF Effect's
> Privacy Policy and instructions: only what you need to coach, never
> outside JF Effect's systems unless the owner says so, and you report any
> suspected privacy breach to the owner immediately.
>
> **5. Professional standards.** Clause A7 applies to you as well: qualified
> scope only, protected titles only if registered, referral when needed.
> You keep [the certifications and insurance the owner requires].
>
> **6. Money.** Unless the owner gives you a billing permission in writing,
> you don't take payments, give refunds, change prices or make promises
> about money to clients. [If the coach collects their own payments under a
> separate arrangement: set out who the merchant is, how JF Effect's share is
> paid, and that the coach's Stripe account is theirs.]
>
> **7. Cleo and AI drafts.** Clause A8 applies: drafts are drafts; you're
> responsible for what you send; significant changes need your confirmation
> and, where the owner requires it, the owner's approval.
>
> **8. Records of what you do.** Actions in JF Effect (access changes, client
> assignments, edits to plans, messages, Cleo actions) are recorded, and the
> owner can review them.
>
> **9. Ending.** When your engagement ends your access is revoked at once;
> clients assigned to you are reassigned by the owner and told by JF Effect.

## C. Data Processing Addendum (Schedule to A)

> **1. Roles.** The coach controls the personal information of their clients
> collected through their organization (the "Client Data"). JF Effect
> processes Client Data on the coach's behalf, only to provide the service and
> as the coach instructs through the software.
>
> **2. JF Effect's commitments.** JF Effect will:
> (a) use Client Data only to provide the service, keep the platform secure
> and meet legal obligations, and not sell it, use it for its own coaching
> business, or use it to train AI models;
> (b) keep it separate from other organizations' data and limit JF Effect
> staff access to what's needed to operate the platform, under an audited,
> time-limited support grant the coach approves (except where a law or an
> urgent security matter requires otherwise, which JF Effect will tell the
> coach about unless the law forbids it);
> (c) protect it with reasonable security safeguards appropriate to its
> sensitivity (encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, logging);
> (d) use only the subprocessors listed in the app (currently: Supabase for
> the database and file storage, OpenAI for AI features, Stripe for payments,
> email and SMS providers, hosting), require equivalent protections from
> them, and give [30] days' notice of a new subprocessor;
> (e) tell the coach without undue delay after becoming aware of a breach of
> security safeguards affecting Client Data, with the information the coach
> needs to assess it and meet its own reporting obligations;
> (f) help the coach answer clients' access, correction and withdrawal
> requests through the software's export and correction tools;
> (g) when the coach's organization closes, delete or de-identify Client Data
> it holds for the organization after the export period, except what the law
> requires JF Effect to keep.
>
> **3. Where data is stored.** Client Data may be stored and processed outside
> Canada (for example in the United States) by JF Effect and its
> subprocessors, where it may be accessible to courts and authorities there.
> The coach will tell their clients so in their own privacy notice.

## D. Upload, import and content rights

Builds on Terms v4 A4 (your content) and A11 (copyright complaints).

**Add to A4:**

> **Files you upload or import.** When you upload a file, import a program
> or meal plan, or paste one in, you confirm you created it or have the
> rights or permission to upload it and use it in JF Effect. Buying a program,
> book or plan usually gives you a licence to use it yourself, not to copy,
> share or sell it; importing a program you bought keeps it private to you
> unless its owner allows more. You keep ownership of what you upload. You
> give JF Effect a limited licence to store, read, convert and show it so you
> can use it in the app (including reading it with AI tools listed in the
> Privacy Policy), and nothing more: we don't make it public, share it with
> other coaches or users, or use it to train AI models. You can delete it at
> any time.

**In-app confirmation (shown before the first upload on any import screen,
unticked, recorded with the import):**

> "I created this or have the rights or permission to upload it and use it in
> JF Effect. Buying a program doesn't by itself give permission to share or
> resell it."

The workout importer already records a confirmation (`IMPORT_RIGHTS_TEXT`,
`program_imports.rights_text`). The nutrition importer and any other upload
surface should use the wording above and record it the same way.

**Add to A11 (copyright complaints):** an in-app "Report copyright" reason on
shared programs and posts (`community_reports` currently has no copyright
reason), routed to the owner with the notice-and-notice information the
Copyright Act requires.

## E. AI assistance disclosure (redline points)

The seeded `ai-assisted-coaching-disclosure` should say, in plain words:

1. Which features use AI: Cleo (for the owner and coaches), drafting
   programs and nutrition plans, reading imported files, estimating a
   meal's macros, interpreting training reports, drafting replies.
2. That AI output is a draft or an estimate. A coach reviews anything that
   reaches a client; an estimate is labelled as one.
3. Which providers process the data (OpenAI; Google only if an admin turns it
   on for a feature) and that they may process it outside Canada; that
   providers are not allowed to train on it [confirm against each provider's
   current API terms].
4. That Cleo isn't a doctor, dietitian or lawyer, doesn't diagnose or treat,
   and won't plan dehydration, diuretics, extreme restriction or unsafe
   weight cuts (these are hard-coded in the app's nutrition and weight
   rules).
5. How to ask for a human: message your coach.

## F. Health information and consent: framework memo

What the app holds that is health-related and sensitive: injuries and pain
reports (body map), conditions, medications, supplements, allergies,
pregnancy and menstrual information where entered, bodyweight and
measurements, progress photos and videos, sleep and recovery, digestion
check-ins, eating-disorder history where disclosed.

1. **Which law applies.** PIPEDA applies to the business's commercial
   collection, use and disclosure of personal information in Manitoba (no
   substantially similar private-sector law there). Alberta, B.C. and Quebec
   have their own private-sector laws for organizations in those provinces;
   Quebec's Law 25 adds consent, impact assessment and breach requirements.
   Manitoba's PHIA applies to "trustees" (health professionals, health care
   facilities, public bodies, health services agencies). A fitness coach who
   isn't a regulated health professional usually isn't a trustee; a coach who
   is (for example a registered dietitian or physiotherapist providing
   services) may be. Don't assume either way: ask counsel per coach type.
2. **Consent.** Health information is sensitive: express consent, specific to
   the purpose (coaching, program and nutrition planning, AI drafting), given
   when it's collected (the Health card, Nutrition Update and pain map each
   say why at the point of entry), with a clear way to withdraw optional uses.
3. **Minimization.** Ask only what coaching needs; free-text health fields
   say not to enter more than needed.
4. **Access and safeguards.** Organization isolation (Phase 3), role-limited
   access, audit records of access changes (built) and of support access
   (planned), encryption in transit and at rest, no health information in
   logs or push notification text (pushes are content-free today).
5. **Retention and deletion.** Account deletion has a 30-day window then
   deletes personal data except what must be kept by law (built). Set and
   publish retention periods for health information after coaching ends.
6. **Access, correction, export.** Build a self-serve export of a person's
   records and files, and a correction request path (Phase 6).
7. **Breaches.** Keep a record of every breach of security safeguards (PIPEDA
   requires records of all breaches for 24 months) and report to the Privacy
   Commissioner and notify affected people when there's a real risk of
   significant harm. Build the internal log and a runbook (Phase 6).
8. **Vendors.** Review each processor's terms for training on customer data,
   storage location and breach notice; list them in the Privacy Policy.
9. **Safety handling (already in the app).** The nutrition and weight systems
   refuse dehydration, diuretics, crash diets and unsafe cuts; screen for
   under-18, pregnancy, eating-disorder history and low BMI before any
   weight-loss plan; refer medical concerns to a doctor or dietitian; and
   never diagnose (pain reports say where it hurts, never what's injured).

## H. Questions for counsel

1. Characterization of independent coaches (A3) and JF Effect's role as
   service provider for their clients' data (A5, C).
2. Enforceability of A13 (cap and carve-outs) and A14 (indemnity) against a
   sole-proprietor coach, and of B2's restrictions on a team coach.
3. Which provincial professional titles are protected where coaches will
   practise (dietitian everywhere; nutritionist, kinesiologist and others
   vary) and the right wording for A7.
4. PHIA (and other provinces' health-information laws) for coaches who are
   regulated health professionals: what changes for those organizations.
5. Whether health information entered by members (no coach) is collected by
   JF Effect as an organization in its own right, and the consent and
   retention that requires.
6. Cross-border processing disclosure wording (C3) for Quebec residents
   under Law 25 (privacy impact assessment for transfers outside Quebec).
7. Stripe Connect: wording of A6 against the Connected Account Agreement and
   whether JF Effect has any merchant obligations with Standard accounts.
8. The copyright complaint process (notice and notice) and whether a
   takedown policy for shared programs is advisable on top of it.
