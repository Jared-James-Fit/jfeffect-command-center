- Logging Level points are awarded only by DB triggers into `athlete_xp_events` with unique per-record/day/week `source_key`s — why: idempotent, no client-side farming, history backfillable.
- Achievement "seen" state lives in `athlete_achievement_views` (server-side), never localStorage — why: reveals must not replay across devices.
- Member-made chats (`chat_groups.kind` = `'direct'` 1:1s and `'crew'` groups) are private to their members: staff RLS, group lists and "View as client" must never include them, and request/invite state (accept, join, decline, block, remove, report) changes only through `chat_direct_respond` / `community_message_author` / `crew_*` — why: declines and removals must stay silent (nobody can tell a decline from unread) and reports are the coach's only window in.
